cardlio library probe

A one-time, read-only check for "My cards on the web". It reads your own cardlio library from your iCloud and reports how it is stored: field names, field types, counts and sizes. It never shows a name, an e-mail, a number or a photo, and it writes nothing.

Sign in with the Apple Account your cardlio library syncs to, press Run, then copy the report.
