Every business card your team shared from cardlio, on any computer: search the whole fair's haul, claim the leads that are yours, and take them with you as vCard or CSV.

Use the Apple ID you use with cardlio on your iPhone or Mac.
  • Apple's sign-in, not ours. Your password goes to Apple only. This site has no accounts and no server.
  • Only your teams. The page reads the teams you made or were invited to, and nothing else from your iCloud.
  • No tracking. No analytics. Your browser keeps only Apple's sign-in, so you stay signed in, and the only connection is to Apple's iCloud.
